# Project Plan — Retail Business Performance Analysis

## 1. Business context

A UK-based online retailer sells gift products to customers in multiple countries. Management wants a clearer understanding of transaction patterns to support decisions about product focus, sales planning, and customer-market priorities.

This is a historical case study based on transactions recorded between 1 December 2010 and 9 December 2011. It is not a claim about the retailer's current performance.

## 2. Problem statement

Management has transaction records but needs a structured, evidence-based view of which products, markets, and periods contribute most to recorded sales, alongside an understanding of cancellations, returns, and data limitations.

## 3. Stakeholders

- Business owner / senior management
- Sales and merchandising team
- Marketing team
- Operations team

## 4. Project objectives

1. Understand the structure and quality of the transaction data.
2. Define transparent measures for recorded revenue and transaction activity.
3. Compare recorded revenue across products, countries, and months.
4. Investigate cancellations, returns, missing values, and unusual records.
5. Translate findings into practical, appropriately qualified recommendations.

## 5. Scope

### In scope
- Descriptive analysis of transactions
- Data-quality assessment
- Product, country, and time-based comparisons
- Visual communication of findings
- Business recommendations supported by observed evidence

### Out of scope
- Predicting future sales
- Claiming profitability or profit margins
- Causal claims about why customers purchased
- Treating historical results as current market conditions

## 6. PACE workflow

### PLAN
- Finalize the business question and stakeholder needs.
- Identify the dataset, variables, and limitations.
- Define the initial analytical questions.

### ANALYZE
- Inspect dimensions, column names, data types, and summary statistics.
- Check missing values, duplicates, cancellations, returns, and invalid prices/quantities.
- Decide and document how each issue will be handled.

### CONSTRUCT
- Create clearly defined analytical fields such as line revenue.
- Build product, country, and monthly summaries.
- Create visualizations and validate calculations.

### EXECUTE
- Present the key findings in plain language.
- Explain limitations and uncertainty.
- Recommend practical next steps and identify what additional data would improve decisions.

## 7. Success criteria

The project is successful when:
- The analysis is reproducible from the documented source data.
- Cleaning choices are explained and justified.
- Metrics are defined clearly and calculated consistently.
- Each major conclusion is supported by a table or visualization.
- Recommendations do not exceed what the dataset can establish.
